About Zzapkart

Zzapkart is an online marketplace where verified Indian sellers and brands sell fashion, electronics, home, beauty, grocery, toys and more. Every seller completes a KYC check before they can sell, and every listing goes through a quality check before it goes live.

Many of our sellers also run their own Brand Store on Zzapkart — you can browse their full range, and checkout, delivery and support stay with Zzapkart.

Orders & tracking

  • Log in and open My Orders to see every order and its status: Placed → Shipped → Delivered.
  • When your order ships, you will see the courier name and tracking ID on the order.
  • An order with items from different sellers ships in separate packages — each one is tracked on its own.
  • To cancel an order before it ships, message us from the chat and include your order ID.

Shipping & delivery

  • Free delivery on orders of ₹499 or more. Below that, delivery is ₹40.
  • Standard delivery: usually 3–5 days after dispatch.
  • Express delivery: ₹99, about 1 day after dispatch where available.
  • Sellers dispatch most orders within 1–3 days. The expected delivery date is shown on the product page and at checkout.

Returns & refunds

  • Most products have a 7-day return window from delivery. Some sellers offer 10 or 30 days — this is shown on the product page and in the seller's store.
  • Items must be unused, with original tags and packaging. Innerwear, personal care and customised items cannot be returned for hygiene reasons.
  • Received a damaged or wrong item? Tell us within 48 hours of delivery and we will arrange a replacement or a full refund.
  • Refunds are issued within 5–7 business days after pickup — to the original payment method for prepaid orders, or to your bank account / UPI for cash on delivery.

Payments

  • We accept UPI, debit and credit cards, and cash on delivery.
  • All payments are processed on Zzapkart's secure checkout. Sellers never see your card or UPI details.
  • Prices include all taxes. Delivery charges, if any, are shown before you pay.

This is a demo website: checkout does not take real payments.

Privacy policy

We collect the information you give us — your name, email, phone number and delivery addresses — to process orders, deliver them and provide support. We share your name, address and phone number only with the seller and courier handling your order.

  • We never sell your personal information.
  • Your password is stored only as a secure hash.
  • You can update your profile and addresses at any time from My Account.

In this demo, your data is stored only in your own browser.

Terms of use

  • Products are sold by independent sellers. Zzapkart provides the platform, payments, delivery tracking and customer support.
  • Prices and stock can change. If a product is priced incorrectly or goes out of stock, the order may be cancelled and any payment refunded in full.
  • Reviews, images and content you share must be your own and must not be offensive or misleading.
  • Sellers must follow our listing guidelines and applicable Indian laws, including consumer protection and Legal Metrology rules.
